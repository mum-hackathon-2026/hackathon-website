Judging Guidelines & Protocol Notes

  • Score each criterion independently and do not reward the same evidence twice.
  • Base scores on what is demonstrated, submitted, or clearly explained in the repo, slides, and demo video.
  • Use the problem statement consistently; there is one problem statement for the event.
  • The performance labels (Weak, Developing, Strong, Excellent) are guidance. Judges may award any whole or fractional number within the relevant range.
  • Technology Integration (TBC) is provisional and will be finalised after sponsor alignment.
